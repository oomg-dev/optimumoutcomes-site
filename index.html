Who we are

Practitioners, not presenters

James Perry, Founder

James spent 21 years as a Probation and Parole Officer with the Virginia Department of Corrections in Fairfax County. Before that he worked in juvenile delinquency counseling, substance abuse counseling, and juvenile group home administration in Washington, D.C. He holds a psychology degree from Northwestern University.

He is a Certified Workforce Development Professional, certified by the National Institute of Corrections to train in:

  • Evidence-based practices
  • Team building
  • Cultural diversity
  • Multi-generation communication
  • Cognitive restructuring

He co-developed a recidivism prevention program and co-sponsors the annual Multi-Cultural Forum in Fairfax County. He has trained staff for corrections and juvenile justice agencies in Virginia and Maryland.

Susan Conner, Training Partner

Susan works in evidence-based practices and brings years of experience with motivational interviewing, feedback, and self-care. She partners with James on program design and delivery.
